Program summary. 36-month plan (kickoff Oct 5, 2026 → close ~Nov 2029) moving from L1 Support Engineer through L2 Engineer and Network & Cybersecurity Specialist toward Solutions Architect — this program's 36-month goal — covering both remote MSP support and field network technician work. vCIO is the next step in a longer 5-year arc beyond this program, not a second Year 3 capstone alongside it. Year 1 (through Nov 2027) targets nine certifications — AZ-900, MS-900, CCST Networking, CCST Cybersecurity, CCT Collaboration, CCT Routing & Switching, CCNA 200-301, AZ-104, MD-102 — and closes out as an L1→L2/Systems Engineer bridge. Years 2–3 add AB-650, AI-901, Security+, AZ-800/AZ-801, and AZ-305, building toward the Solutions Architect capstone; vCIO Competency Track reps (technology-roadmap delivery, QBR practice, vendor-negotiation reps) continue in parallel as groundwork for that later step, not as the Year 3 focus itself.

Future-proofing pass (added 2026-09-13, §29 in the reference doc). Both target roles are shifting under industry research already folded into this calendar: architects moving from hands-on design toward AI/agent-system orchestration and governance (Microsoft's own new AB-100 credential is built around exactly this), and vCIOs needing real technical-financial fluency for AI-specific cost dynamics (IDC's FinOps-for-AI forecasting). Practical effect on this plan: the homelab AI Gateway build (Y2 Q3) now includes basic agent governance/RBAC from day one, the Year 2 roadmap draft gets an AI cost/ROI section, and two new skill domains (26–27, below) are tracked pending your self-assessment.

This page is the working control center; the full reasoning, hour estimates, and risk register live in the companion Master Training Program reference document.
